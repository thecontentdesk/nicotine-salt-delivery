The Science Behind Nicotine Salt Formulation

Nicotine Salts and Their Chemical Structure

Nicotine salts form when nicotine combines with an organic acid. Manufacturers use this process to change the chemical properties of the e-liquid. Benzoic acid is one example of an acid used in nicotine salt formulations.

This formulation generally has a lower pH than freebase nicotine. However, the exact composition depends on the manufacturer and product formulation. Nicotine concentration and chemical composition are important factors when evaluating any nicotine-containing product.

The Role of Formulation Consistency

E-liquid formulation involves more than nicotine alone. It can include flavour ingredients, solvents, and other compounds. Manufacturers must consider how these ingredients interact during storage and heating.

Consistent formulation helps maintain product quality. However, it does not automatically establish product safety. Heated e-liquid can produce substances that may affect respiratory health.

Nicotine Delivery and Aerosol Formation

From Liquid Formulation to Aerosol
